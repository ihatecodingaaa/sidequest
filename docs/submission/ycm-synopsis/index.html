The problem

The difficult moment happens before the offence.

Many youths already know that stealing, misusing an account or following a risky dare is wrong. The difficulty is deciding safely while it is happening and friends are watching.

Retailer side prevention is working, measurably. The gap SIDEQUEST addresses is the youth side decision moment, which a retailer programme cannot reach.

SIDEQUEST, Content and Experience Synopsis The problem 2 / 11

SIDEQUEST Streets

Crime prevention becomes somewhere you can explore.

Participants enter a fictional Singapore inspired neighbourhood rather than opening a directory of lessons. District 01 has shopfronts, a void deck, a kopitiam, a court and a community post, and every building that opens is the front door of something a participant can actually do.

The world is the interaction layer. Mission state, XP, the Safety Passport and every safety rule stay deterministic underneath it, in the same product logic that runs without the world. Delete Streets and every point earned is still valid.

SIDEQUEST, Content and Experience Synopsis Streets, alive 4 / 11

Core mechanics

Four ways to practise prevention.

REWIND

Play the scene, reach an outcome, then return to the decision that produced it and take a different route. Both outcomes stay on screen.

Norm Mirror

Predict what most people your age would do, then say what you would do. The gap between them is the misperception peer pressure runs on.

BREAKSAFE

Prevention as engineering. Find what makes the honest action hard at a self checkout, then change it.

Crew Shift

Two private rounds, one either side of a group discussion. The second round is both the after distribution and the crew decision.

Crew Shift runs inside Chapter 4 of the ONE BAD MINUTE campaign, not as a standalone mode. Played alone it offers a Solo preview whose example answers are written down rather than simulated, labelled throughout, and counted for nothing.

None of the four has a correct answer or awards a score.

Prevention Threads and Signals

One situation can connect people, places and decisions.

The favour

Devi is asked to lend her bank login to a friend, just for a week. Five steps across the void deck, the bus stop and the court: hear what she was asked, ask someone who refused first, ask an adult who knows what follows, then decide what to say. Every option carries an honest consequence and none costs XP.

Prevention Signals
CONNECT Somebody needs information, a trusted adult, or an official source.
PREVENT Nothing has gone wrong yet, and there is a way to make this safer.
REDIRECT Pressure is building. It can still be interrupted.
PROTECT Someone may not be safe. Distance, support, and get help.

A Signal marks a situation, never a person. There is no risk field on anybody in this product.

Colour is one of four channels. The silhouette is the one that survives a red-green colour vision deficiency, and the label travels into the Quest List.

No profiling, no vigilante gameplay, no confrontation.

SIDEQUEST, Content and Experience Synopsis Threads and Signals 6 / 11

Youth participation

Do not turn youths into police. Help them become capable peers.

Scout Notices a situation early, while it is still small enough to change.
Ally Stays with the person it is happening to, which is usually what they needed.
Connector Brings in the adult, the staff member or the official channel.
Designer Changes the place so the situation is less likely next time.
Creator Makes the version of this that other young people will actually watch.

These are participation identities, not law enforcement ranks. A role is a view of a capability the profile already holds. Nothing is unlocked for it and a role cannot be lost.

The Community Safety Crew has roles and no powers. Nobody in it is police or pretending to be.

SIDEQUEST, Content and Experience Synopsis Youth participation 7 / 11

Rewards and integrity

Recognition, not currency.

XP

XP is progression and identity, not a balance. It is awarded once per mission, claiming costs nothing, and nothing is ever deducted.

Rewards

Partner rewards are bounded participation milestones. No reward scales with incident severity.

Proposed ceiling

Up to S$10 per participant across the pilot, subject to confirmed in kind sponsorship.

No merchant partnership is confirmed at submission. Any retailer shown in the prototype is illustrative, and the product states so on the screen where the claim is made.

Guardrails
  • Safe sits outside reward logic entirely.
  • No profiling, no individual risk scores, no public crime map.
  • No reward for reporting, photographing or identifying anybody.
  • Every prototype aggregate is labelled as prototype.
SIDEQUEST, Content and Experience Synopsis Rewards and integrity 8 / 11

Six month pilot

Six months, one honest starting position.

Starting position

Participants to date: zero. Data collected: none. Organisations engaged: none. Everything below starts from there.

Nov 2026 Co-design and partner preparation
Dec 2026 Dry run, then Pilot Wave 1
Jan to Feb 2027 Iteration, then Pilot Wave 2
Mar 2027 Activation
Apr 2027 Evaluation

Target: approximately 150 secondary and post-secondary youths.

Sessions run a fortnight apart rather than in one sitting, because multiple sessions is one of the three moderators the serious games literature identifies, and a single session tests the wrong thing.

The largest scheduling risk is Crew Shift, which needs real groups of three or four. If it is not run with real groups, the pilot has tested the solo product and will say so.

SIDEQUEST, Content and Experience Synopsis Six month pilot 9 / 11

Measurement

Measure what SIDEQUEST can reasonably influence.

MeasureWhat it asksExpected
Risk recognition Names what is developing before it resolves. Facilitator scored. Upward
Intervention confidence Confidence in responding safely, across three scenarios. Upward
Escalation route Knows 999 and the emergency SMS route. Clearest movement
Perceived peer norms Distance between what I would do and what I think others would. Gap narrows
Willingness to redirect Said something to a friend about something risky. Upward, weakly
Behavioural traces Steps completed, replays with a different choice, return visits. Rises across sessions

The pilot measures proximal behavioural and learning outcomes. It does not claim that a small pilot directly reduces the crime rate in Singapore. Without a comparison group the evaluation is descriptive.

Every measure is anonymous and aggregate. No names of other people are collected, ever.

“Not enough for a claim about effect.”
